D harmonic minor scale on the bandoneon

Notes: D · E · F · G · A · B♭ · C♯ · D (T S T T S 1½ S)

The harmonic minor is the natural minor with a raised 7th degree (the leading note). Between the 6th and 7th degrees there is a tone and a half, a sound you hear a lot in tango. The natural version (without the raised 7th) is D natural minor.

T = tone, S = semitone, 1½ = tone and a half

Each map shows the scale going up one octave in one bellows direction: the numbers give the order of the notes and the colour shows the bellows (blue opening, orange closing). When a note does not exist in the map's direction, the bellows change just for that note. Each note uses the button nearest the previous one, as the app suggests. It is not a fingering: agree the fingers with your teacher or follow your method.
