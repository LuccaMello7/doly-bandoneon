The staff and the clefs

The staff has five lines. Each line and each space between them is a note: the higher it sits, the higher it sounds. The clef at the start tells you which note goes where.

On the bandoneon, the right hand usually reads in the treble clef and the left in the bass clef. In the treble clef, the lines from bottom to top are E, G, B, D, F and the spaces F, A, C, E. In the bass clef, the lines are G, B, D, F, A. Notes outside the staff use ledger lines, small extra dashes.
