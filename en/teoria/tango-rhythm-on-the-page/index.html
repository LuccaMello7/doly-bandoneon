Tango rhythm on the page

Tango accompaniment revolves around a few patterns, and each has its own way of being written. Marcato in 4 is four short chords per bar, accented on 1 and 3. Marcato in 2 marks only 1 and 3, with rests in between.

Syncopation moves the accent onto the off-beat, before the strong beat. 3-3-2 groups the eight quavers of the bar as 3 + 3 + 2 and is usually written as two dotted crotchets and a crotchet.

Why it matters on the bandoneon

In tango, the bandoneon is also percussion: the left hand marks the rhythm with low chords and the bellows give each accent its weight. In the Rhythm tab you practise these same patterns in time, tapping along.
