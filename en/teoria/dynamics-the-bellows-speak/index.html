Dynamics: the bellows speak

Dynamics are how loud the music is: loud, soft and the way from one to the other. Music uses Italian letters: p (piano, soft), f (forte, loud), mp and mf (mezzo, in between), pp and ff (very soft and very loud).

There are also signs for change: the crescendo, an opening sign (<), and the decrescendo, a closing one (>). An accent (> over a note) brings out a single note, and sforzando (sfz) is a strong, sudden attack.

Why it matters on the bandoneon

On the bandoneon, the bellows make the dynamics: more pressure, more sound. The fingers only choose the notes; pressing a button harder does not make it louder. So p, f, crescendos and accents become movements of the arm and the bellows.
