The major scale

A scale is a series of notes in order, from the tonic up to the same note an octave higher. The major scale always follows the same pattern of whole steps (W) and half steps (H): W W H W W W H.

Starting on C, it uses only the natural notes: C, D, E, F, G, A, B, C, with the half steps between E–F and B–C. Starting on another note, some degrees need a sharp or a flat to keep the pattern: G major has F♯ and F major has B♭.

Why it matters on the bandoneon

On the bandoneon, each scale has a path under the hand for each bellows direction: starting on C or on G changes the buttons and sometimes the most comfortable direction. In the Scale map and Guided scales you can see and play the path of each one.
