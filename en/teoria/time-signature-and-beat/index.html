Time signature and beat

Music is organised in bars (measures), separated by bar lines. The fraction at the start tells you how many beats fit in each bar (the top number) and which note value gets one beat (the bottom; 4 = crotchet).

Tango is usually written in 2/4 or 4/4. The first beat of each bar is the strongest. BPM tells you how many beats fit in a minute: 60 BPM is one beat per second.
