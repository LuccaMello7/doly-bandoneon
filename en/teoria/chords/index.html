Triads: major and minor

A chord is three or more notes played together. A triad stacks two thirds: the root, the third and the fifth.

In a major triad (C, E, G) the lower third is major (4 semitones) and the upper one minor (3). In a minor triad (A, C, E) it is the other way round. With two minor thirds, the triad is diminished (B, D, F).

Why it matters on the bandoneon

In tango, the bandoneon’s left hand usually plays the chords, short in marcato or held. Each chord has one shape opening and another closing: on the left hand, only 2 of the 33 buttons give the same note in both directions.
