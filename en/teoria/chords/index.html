Chords

A chord is three or more notes played together, stacked in thirds. A major chord has the root, the major third and the fifth (C, E, G) and sounds bright. A minor chord has the minor third (A, C, E) and sounds sadder. A seventh chord adds one more note (G, B, D, F) and "asks" to resolve.

In tango, the left hand usually plays the bass on the strong beat and the chord on the other beats.
