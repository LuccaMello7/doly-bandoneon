Notes and octaves

Music uses seven note names that repeat: C, D, E, F, G, A and B (in Latin countries, Do, Re, Mi, Fa, Sol, La, Si). After B comes C again, one octave higher.

The number after the name gives the octave: C4 is "middle C", in the middle of the piano. In BanDoly you choose in Settings whether to see C D E or Do Re Mi.
