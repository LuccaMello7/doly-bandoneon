For bandoneon teachers

Your student practises at home. You see how it went.

BanDoly is a bandoneon app with the full keyboard and the recorded sound of a real instrument. For teachers, it works as a homework book: you send the exercise by link and get a report on what the student played.

Create an assignmentSee how it works

No sign-up on either side. BanDoly is currently free.

How it works

  1. 1

    You set up the assignment

    Choose an Academy exercise or one of your own scores, the practice mode and the speed. You can put up to 6 exercises in a plan and set a due date.

  2. 2

    The student opens the link

    Send it by WhatsApp or email. The student taps the link and finds the exercise ready, with your message, on a phone or a computer.

  3. 3

    You get the report

    At the end, the student sends the report back. You open the link and see the score, the rhythm, the bellows direction and the notes they got right and wrong.

The report you receive

Each exercise comes with the score, three measures and a map, from the first note to the last. That way you know where the student got stuck before the lesson starts.

What else you can do

  • Your fingering goes with it

    Mark the finger for each note in the Academy and the assignment carries your fingering. The app shows the fingers you chose, but does not check which finger the student used.

  • Your own scores

    Import a score in MusicXML or MIDI and send it as an assignment: the student receives the music already mapped to the bandoneon buttons.

  • With the real instrument

    Anyone with a bandoneon can switch on the microphone: the app listens to the instrument and checks the note and the rhythm. The button and the bellows direction are only checked on the on-screen keyboard.

  • Everything inside the link

    The assignment and the report travel inside the links themselves. Neither you nor the student needs an account, and nothing goes through a server.

Teachers' questions

  • How much does it cost?

    BanDoly is currently free.

  • Does BanDoly replace lessons?

    No. It supports practice between one lesson and the next, and it does not correct posture, tone or fingering.

  • Does the student need to own a bandoneon?

    No. They can study where the notes are and how the bellows work on the on-screen keyboard. With the instrument in hand, practice goes further.

  • Does it work on my phone?

    Yes: in the browser on a phone, tablet or computer. It can also be installed as an app.

  • Can I use it with students under 18?

    Yes. The assignment link and the report need no account. Only the optional BanDoly account is for people aged 18 or over.

  • In which languages?

    Portuguese, Spanish and English.
