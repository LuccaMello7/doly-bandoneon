Who is Doly Costa?

Doly Carlos da Costa: a life devoted to the bandoneon and to gaúcho culture.

Born in Caçapava do Sul, Doly Carlos da Costa built a career devoted to music, to the bandoneon and to the traditions of Rio Grande do Sul, in southern Brazil.

From his first performances in dance orchestras to the nativist music festivals, his story brings together talent, dedication and cultural recognition.

Today, at 97, Doly still plays the bandoneon.

Timeline

Key moments and honors of his artistic career.

  1. 1940sFirst steps in music

    While still a teenager, Doly began playing in a dance orchestra in Caçapava do Sul, inspired by the bandoneon his father played.

  2. Around 1951Tango orchestras in Porto Alegre

    At 22 he moved to Porto Alegre, the state capital, where he played in the tango orchestras of Rádio Farroupilha and Rádio Gaúcha.

  3. 1977Founding Os Posteiros

    He returned to music and founded the group Os Posteiros, taking a bigger part in regional music and nativist festivals.

  4. September 17, 2001Simões Lopes Neto Medal

    The Government of the State of Rio Grande do Sul awarded him the Simões Lopes Neto Medal in recognition of his outstanding work in the field of culture.

  5. 2011Vitor Mateus Teixeira Award

    He was honored in the Instrumentalist category by the Legislative Assembly of Rio Grande do Sul.

  6. 2011Tribute at the 25th Moenda da Canção

    He was honored during the festival's anniversary edition in Santo Antônio da Patrulha.

The legacy of Doly Carlos da Costa

Doly's career is an important contribution to preserving and spreading the bandoneon in Rio Grande do Sul.

His work in tango orchestras, the founding of Os Posteiros and the honors he received throughout his career show his deep bond with regional music and gaúcho cultural identity.

Among these honors stands out the Simões Lopes Neto Medal, awarded in 2001, which records the State's official recognition of his cultural work.

And the story goes on: at 97, Doly still plays his bandoneon.
