A♭7 chord on the bandoneon

Notes: A♭ · C · E♭ · G♭ (1 · 3 · 5 · ♭7)

A dominant seventh chord combines the root, the major third, the fifth and the minor seventh. When all four notes do not fit the hand, the shape without the fifth also works. Also written G♯7.

Each map shows one side of the bandoneon, with the buttons where they sit on the instrument. The lit buttons form the chord in the bellows direction shown: blue for opening, orange for closing.

We show the most compact shape, with the root in the bass where possible. The app has other positions and a chord practice mode.
