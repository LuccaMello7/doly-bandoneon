What is the bandoneon

The bandoneon is a free-reed bellows instrument from the concertina family. It was developed in Germany around 1840 and is named after Heinrich Band, an instrument dealer from Krefeld who helped spread it.

It was first used in German religious and folk music. At the end of the 19th century it reached the Río de la Plata (Buenos Aires and Montevideo) with immigrants and became the voice of tango.

Some names to listen to: Pedro Maffia, Pedro Laurenz, Aníbal Troilo, Astor Piazzolla, Leopoldo Federico and Dino Saluzzi.
