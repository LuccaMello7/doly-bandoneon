Two hands, one bellows

Both hands share the same bellows. This has two important consequences:

  1. At any given moment, both hands play in the same direction. A melody note and a bass played together must both exist in that direction.
  2. While a note is sounding, the bellows cannot reverse, or the note changes pitch midway. So direction changes happen when both hands attack together or in a rest.

How to practice: first each hand alone, then both together very slowly. On the Melodies tab, open Ode to Joy with both hands in Wait mode. The app plans the bellows for both hands and shows the score on a grand staff.
