Practice routine

A short daily routine pays off more than hours once a week. The home screen shows your routine for today and how many days in a row you have practiced. A 20–30 minute suggestion:

  1. 5 min: bellows. Long notes opening and closing, at a steady volume.
  2. 10 min: button map. Slow C and G major scales, one hand at a time. Use the Guided scales in Wait mode and the Find the note challenge.
  3. 5 min: rhythm. Marcato and syncopation with a metronome.
  4. 5–10 min: repertoire. A melody or an imported tango excerpt (MusicXML), very slowly, plus the accompaniment chords.

An in-person teacher matters a lot for posture, straps and bellows technique, which an app cannot correct.
