Reading: two clefs

Bandoneon scores use a two-staff system, like the piano:

  • Treble clef: right hand (high notes).
  • Bass clef: left hand (low notes).

In the treble clef, the lines from bottom to top are E, G, B, D, F. In the bass clef, G, B, D, F, A.
