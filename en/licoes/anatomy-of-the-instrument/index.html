Anatomy of the instrument

  • Two boxes (sides), one in each hand, holding the buttons.
  • Bellows in the middle: opening or closing it pushes air through the reeds.
  • Reeds: metal tongues that vibrate. Each note usually sounds two of them, tuned an octave apart, which gives the full tone.
  • Air valve: a lever worked by the right thumb. It lets air in or out silently so you can reposition the bellows.
  • Straps: hold your hands to the boxes.

The classic "Argentine" bandoneon (Rheinische system) has 71 buttons: 38 on the right hand and 33 on the left. Since each button plays two notes, that makes 142 voices.
