Bellows and expression

The bellows is your "violin bow": it controls volume, attack and expression. Your fingers only choose the notes.

  • More bellows pressure: louder sound. Light pressure: piano.
  • Plan where to change direction, ideally between phrases or in rests, the way a singer plans breathing.
  • If the bellows runs out, use the air valve to reposition it silently.
  • Never force the bellows without pressing buttons or the valve: it damages the instrument.

On the Keyboard tab the bellows is simulated: if you play too long in one direction, it runs out. Use Space to switch direction. To reposition the bellows silently, hold ← or A (valve opening) and → or F (valve closing). When the computer keyboard is playing notes, use the arrows.
