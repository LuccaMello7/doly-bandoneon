Chords on the bandoneon

A chord is made of notes stacked in thirds. The major chord has a root, a major third and a fifth (C-E-G). The minor has a minor third (A-C-E). The seventh (dominant) adds a minor seventh (G-B-D-F) and "wants" to resolve to the next chord.

On the bisonoric bandoneon, every chord has one shape opening and another closing, with different buttons. That is why it pays to learn the most common chords in both directions.

In tango accompaniment, the left hand usually plays the bass (root) on the strong beat and the chord on the other beats, or the whole chord in marcato.

On the Chords tab you can see each chord shape on the keyboard, hear it and practice. Start with Am, Dm, E7 and C, very common in tangos in A minor.
