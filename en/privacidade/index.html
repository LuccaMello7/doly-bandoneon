Privacy

BanDoly works without sign-up and without cookies. If you do not create an account, nothing of yours leaves your device. This page explains what happens to your information, with and without an account.

Updated on 10 November 2026.

What stays on your device

Your progress (lessons, challenges, records and practice days) and your preferences (sound, language and button map) are kept in your browser's storage, on your own device. They exist only so the app works and remembers where you left off.

Without an account, this data is not sent to BanDoly or anyone else. You can download a copy or erase everything in Settings, or by clearing the site data in your browser.

Cookies

BanDoly doesn't use cookies, neither for advertising nor for tracking. That is why the site shows no cookie notice.

Visit counting

To know how many people use the app and which pages are visited most, BanDoly uses GoatCounter. It counts visits and some uses of the app (for example, how many times someone starts an exercise or creates an account) in aggregate, without cookies and without storing your IP address, and it cannot identify you.

Microphone, MIDI and files

When you turn on the microphone to play your bandoneon, the sound is analysed in the browser itself. Nothing is recorded or sent over the internet.

The same goes for a MIDI controller and for the scores you import in the Academy: everything is read and kept only on your device.

YouTube videos

Doly Costa's videos are on YouTube and only load when you tap them, in privacy-enhanced mode. From that tap on, YouTube's privacy rules apply.

Teachers' assignments and suggestions

The assignment link a teacher sends carries the exercise in the address itself. The report only leaves the app when you send it yourself, by WhatsApp, email or any other app you choose.

The report link carries inside it the name you typed and the results of the exercises. Whoever receives the link can see that information, and none of it passes through BanDoly. The reports a teacher opens are kept only on the teacher's device.

The Suggestions button opens a message on GitHub, which is public, or copies the text for you to send however you like. Do not write personal data in a suggestion sent through GitHub.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any server, GitHub's records technical access data, such as the IP address, for the security and running of the service, under GitHub's privacy policy.

Account (optional)

The account is optional and only for people aged 18 or over. It keeps a copy of your progress, the melodies you create and your settings in the cloud, so you can carry on where you left off on another device.

To create the account, we store your email address. If you sign in with Google, it also gives us your name and profile picture: we use only your first name, to greet you in the app, and we do not use the picture. We also record when you accepted the Terms of Use and declared that you are 18 or over.

We use this data only to make the account work, which is the service you asked for (art. 7, V, of Brazil’s LGPD). It is not sold, not used for advertising and not shared with anyone other than the services below.

Classes

A teacher with an account can create a class and invite students by link. Only people with an account can join, so classes are also for people aged 18 or over only.

When you join a class, you choose the name the teacher will see. While you are in it, the teacher sees that name, the days you practised and the results of the class assignment exercises. They do not see your email or the rest of your progress, and a student cannot see the other students.

You can leave the class whenever you like, in the Account tab: what was shared with the teacher is erased straight away. The teacher can also remove a student or delete the class. This data is kept in the same database as the account.

Where the account data is kept

The account data is kept on Supabase, the database and sign-in service BanDoly uses, on servers in the city of São Paulo. The database rules only let you read and change your data. Your sign-in is stored in your browser, without cookies.

The emails with the sign-in link are sent through an email delivery service. If you sign in with Google, the sign-in goes through Google, under its privacy policy.

Your rights

The account data is kept for as long as the account exists. In the Account tab you can download a copy of everything in the cloud and delete the account at any time: your sign-in and the cloud data are erased straight away.

For your other rights under the LGPD, such as knowing what data we hold, correcting it or asking about this page, write to contato@bandoly.app.

The person responsible for the data (the controller, under the LGPD) is Lucca Costa, creator of BanDoly.

Questions?
