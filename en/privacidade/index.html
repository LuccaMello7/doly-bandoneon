Privacy

BanDoly doesn't ask you to sign up, doesn't use cookies and doesn't keep your data on a server. This page explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

Updated on 10 November 2026.

What stays on your device

Your progress (lessons, challenges, records and practice days) and your preferences (sound, language and button map) are kept in your browser's storage, on your own device. They exist only so the app works and remembers where you left off.

This data is not sent to BanDoly or to anyone else. You can download a copy or delete everything in Settings, or by clearing the site data in your browser.

Cookies

BanDoly doesn't use cookies, neither for advertising nor for tracking. That is why the site shows no cookie notice.

Visit counting

To know how many people use the app and which pages are visited most, BanDoly uses GoatCounter. It counts visits in aggregate, without cookies and without storing your IP address, and it cannot identify you.

Microphone, MIDI and files

When you turn on the microphone to play your bandoneon, the sound is analysed in the browser itself. Nothing is recorded or sent over the internet.

The same goes for a MIDI controller and for the scores you import in the Academy: everything is read and kept only on your device.

YouTube videos

Doly Costa's videos are on YouTube and only load when you tap them, in privacy-enhanced mode. From that tap on, YouTube's privacy rules apply.

Teachers' assignments and suggestions

The assignment link a teacher sends carries the exercise in the address itself. The report only leaves the app when you send it yourself, by WhatsApp, email or any other app you choose.

The Suggestions button opens a message on GitHub, which is public, or copies the text for you to send however you like. Do not write personal data in a suggestion sent through GitHub.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any server, GitHub's records technical access data, such as the IP address, for the security and running of the service, under GitHub's privacy policy.

If there is ever an account

There is no sign-up today. If BanDoly starts offering an account to keep your progress in the cloud, it will be optional, this page will be updated first, and you will be able to download and delete your data whenever you like.

Questions

As BanDoly doesn't keep your personal data on a server, BanDoly holds no data of yours to view, correct or delete: it is all on your device. For questions about this page, use the Suggestions button in the app.
