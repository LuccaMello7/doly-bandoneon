Bandoneon notes: a map of all 71 buttons

The bandoneon is bisonoric: each button plays one note when the bellows open and another when they close. That is why the map shows two notes per button.

How to read it: each circle is a button, in the position it has on the instrument (as on the app keyboard). On top, in blue, the note when opening the bellows; below, in orange, the note when closing.

● opening● closing

Map of the 142-voice Rheinische system, the most common in tango, transcribed from the diagrams by Omar Caccia and Matías Gobbo. Some instruments differ slightly: in the app, you can edit the map.
